SayWhen.am — AI voice agents for business calls

Conversational voice agents that pick up and place phone calls for a business — no phone menus, no bigger team.

What the product does

  • Inbound and outbound calls held as a real conversation, not a script.
  • Natural routing: the agent understands intent from speech and resolves the request or passes it to the right person.
  • Connected to the client's data: CRM, knowledge base and call history, so answers are accurate and personal.
  • Scheduling straight into the calendar during the call.
  • Live transcripts and analytics on a dashboard.

Different verticals, different setups

Each vertical gets its own combination of speech and language models, voice, scripts, integrations and modules: support hotlines, sales and lead qualification, appointment booking and reminders, high-volume call routing. For an insurance company we scoped a retrieval-based assistant (RAG) that answers questions from the insurer's own documents.

What I do

  • Discovery: map who calls, why, and what a good outcome looks like for each client.
  • Specs and backlog: turn client needs into requirements and priorities with our CTO and partners.
  • Vibe-coding and features: build and test features myself with AI coding agents, and decide where a quick build is enough and where an engineer is needed.
  • Delivery: keep milestones, check each stage against the client's business requirements and keep clients updated.
