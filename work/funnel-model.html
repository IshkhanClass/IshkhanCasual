Client-acquisition funnel model

For a B2B consultancy entering the U.S. market I designed a 12-week outbound pilot and built a Python model that calculates it and generates the report and slide decks. The client and its numbers stay confidential; this page shows how the model works.

QuestionWhich buying signal brings the most calls per dollar spent?
Built withClaude Code, Python; report and decks generated from one model.
Key findingOne mid-size sale pays back the whole pilot, so quality of each touch beats volume.

How the model works

  1. Signals: three buying triggers — a fresh funding round, hiring in sales, a new sales or marketing leader — scored on volume, detectability, intent and fit.
  2. Accounts and tiers: weekly accounts per trigger; tiers decide how personal each touch is.
  3. Funnel: contacts → positive replies → calls → first sale → larger engagement, with conversion rates per stage.
  4. Scenarios: conservative, base and optimistic, so the spread is visible before anyone commits money.
  5. Costs and payback: tools and people, cost per call, cost per client and the break-even point.
  6. Decision gates: checks at weeks 3, 6, 8 and 12 — keep, fix, replace the trigger or stop.
